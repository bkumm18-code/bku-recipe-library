# Myanmar holiday input

Pass official year-specific Myanmar public-holiday dates as an ISO-date JSON array. Include Thingyan, Union Day, Peasants’ Day, Martyrs’ Day, National Day, Christmas, New Year, and full-moon holidays (Kasone, Waso, Thadingyut, Tazaungdaing). Dates vary by year; use an authoritative current government or embassy calendar.
